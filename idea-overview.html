What Is It?
FinSakhi (फिनसखी) is a full-stack AI-powered financial literacy platform bridging India's knowledge gap. It combines adaptive assessments, personalized e-learning, an LLM chatbot, investment tracking, government scheme discovery, AI audio podcasts, and a gamified RPG — in Hindi & English.
Problem Statement
780 million Indians lack basic financial literacy. Rural communities face language barriers, complex jargon, and zero personalized guidance — leading to informal moneylenders, missed govt. benefits, and poor decisions.

Our Objective & Proposed Solution
To design a one-stop, AI-native financial companion that meets rural and semi-urban Indians exactly where they are — on their phone, in their language — with tools that teach, guide, and empower. FinSakhi adapts to each user's income, occupation, and literacy level, turning complex financial concepts into everyday stories, interactive quizzes, podcasts, and practical action steps. Rather than a generic app, FinSakhi is a personalized Sakhi (friend) who walks alongside the user on their entire financial journey.
AI Assessment
Adaptive 8-MCQ literacy test via Groq Llama 3.3 — personalized to income, family & occupation.
FinLearning
4 Pillars × 3 Levels of LLM-personalized story-based lessons with real-time health score tracking.
AI Mentor
Context-aware chatbot that knows your profile, assessment history, learning progress & investments.
AI Podcasts
Auto-generated two-host audio podcasts per lesson using Groq LLM + MS Edge Neural TTS.
Investment Hub
Real-time commodity prices, mutual fund NAVs, portfolio tracker & AI credit card recommendations.
Govt. Schemes
AI-matched government schemes (PM-KISAN, Jan Dhan, Sukanya Samriddhi, PMSBY) by user profile.
FinGame RPG
Story-driven financial simulation RPG where choices teach budgeting, saving & investing through consequences.
Gamification
XP points, level-up system, financial health scores, login streaks & revision mode to drive retention.
